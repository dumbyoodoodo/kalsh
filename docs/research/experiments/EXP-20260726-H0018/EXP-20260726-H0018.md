# EXP-20260726-H0018 — Weather vs. Kalshi market probability (preliminary baseline)

**Hypothesis:** H0018 (see HYPOTHESES.md). **Run:** 2026-07-26.
**Analysis commit:** recorded in `results.json` (`grain_sha256`).
**Result artifacts:** `results.json`, `sample.csv`, `descriptive_predictions.csv`.

## Outcome: coverage-blocked → descriptive-only

The pre-registered held-out test is **not evaluable**. At the 24 h horizon, the
as-of **forecast** join leaves only **162 markets / 14 event-days spanning
2026-07-21→07-25** (840 markets excluded for having no forecast issued at ≤ the
decision time; forecast collection is recent and sparse). Every survivor falls
inside the pre-registered *test* window (07-13→07-25), so **train and validation
are empty** and no weather model can be fit on held-out data.

Per H0018's pre-registered claim-reduction clause, the run falls back to a
**descriptive in-sample** fit on all 162 rows, clearly flagged
(`held_out_test_evaluable=false`). In-sample fitting is *biased in favour of the
weather model*, so it is the most generous possible look.

## Integrity

Leakage checks all pass: every price/forecast/observation timestamp is ≤ the
decision time; the decision time is < close; no liquidity feature and no
result-derived feature is present. The build is deterministic (identical
`grain_sha256` and bootstrap across re-runs).

## Descriptive metrics (in-sample; not a test)

**M3 − M1 Brier (in-sample, event-grouped bootstrap, 2000×, seed 20260726):**
+0.0002, 95% CI [−0.0089, +0.0087] (spans 0). Subgroups sign-inconsistent
(DEN −0.016, LAX +0.011, CHI +0.004, NYC +0.002), driven by the Denver-heavy
sample.

## Interpretation

1. **The raw market is well-calibrated** (slope 1.13, ECE 0.052) and far better
   than the base rate (0.111 vs 0.139).
2. **Weather-only has weak signal** — worse than the market (0.133 vs 0.111).
3. **Adding weather does not improve the market** — M3 ≈ M1 (+0.0002), *even
   in-sample* where overfitting should favour M3; the CI spans 0.
4. Any apparent improvement is confined to Denver and is subgroup-unstable.
5. **No tradable conclusion** — probability scoring only; no execution/fees/
   liquidity; tiny, Denver-dominated, coverage-blocked sample.

## Verdict

**NOT SUPPORTED — MARKET BENCHMARK NOT BEATEN**, with the explicit caveat that
the pre-registered *held-out* test could not be run (forecast coverage), so this
rests on a descriptive in-sample analysis that is biased toward the weather
model and still shows no improvement. The dominant limitation is **forecast
history length**: the honest next step is to accumulate point-in-time forecast
coverage and re-run the *unchanged* pre-registered experiment once a real
train/test split is populated.
