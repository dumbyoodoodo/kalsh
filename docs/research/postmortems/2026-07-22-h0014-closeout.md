## 2. Adjudication of the three candidate explanations

**1. Ordinary sampling variation — rejected for CHI tmax, confirmed for
everything else.** Season-matching was the sharpest test available:
CHI's historical Jan–Jul tmax rate is 15.6% (higher than its 13.9%
full-year rate — seasonality is real and explains part of the raw
full-year comparison that motivated H0014), yet the 2026 elevation
survives it decisively at the multiplicity-corrected level. The other
three cells are unremarkable: NYC's 2026 gap (+9.0pp) sits almost
exactly on its own historical mean (+9.4pp).

**2. Temporary operational change — no supporting evidence.** All
registered indicators are clean at CHI: cadence 2.020 vs 2.008
issuances/variable-day (diff 0.012, threshold 0.15); missing-day
fraction 2.0% vs 1.5% (diff 0.5pp, threshold 5pp); single-issuance
fraction 0.5% vs 0.7%. Archive and observation completeness are
excellent in every window (≥98%); no collection anomaly is visible in
any registered diagnostic. Per the frozen rule, no unregistered
explanation was invented to fill the gap.

**3. Genuine regime shift — possible but uncorroborated.** The monthly
profile shows the CHI tmax elevation is broad (elevated in 5 of 7
months, peaks in March/April/June — fig4), not a single-event artifact;
but NYC, whose reporting pipeline and weather regime overlap CHI's,
shows nothing. A genuine process change (e.g. an NWS Chicago-office
practice change) affecting one station is plausible and cannot be
distinguished from a station-local statistical fluctuation at n = 198
— which is exactly what outcome B says.

## 3. What this means for prior findings

- **The asymmetry's *current-day* magnitude at CHI is unreliable**: the

## 6. Limitations

1. **n = 198 in every 2026 cell**: only ~8pp-scale shifts were
   decidable; the three "no shift" cells bound nothing smaller.
2. **CHI's shift finding is partially post-hoc** (disclosed pre-freeze,
   PREREG §0): CHI's full-year cells motivated the hypothesis. The
   season-matched window and multiplicity-corrected level are genuine
   hardening, but the strongest unbiased statement is NYC's stability,
   which was fully unseen.
3. **Operational indicators cover the archive, not the NWS office**: a
   reporting-practice change at NWS Chicago that alters revision
   behavior without changing cadence/completeness would look exactly
   like this. Raw CLI product text for CHI 2026 (stored in
   `raw_api_payloads`) is the place a deeper operational investigation
   would look — flagged as follow-up work, not performed here (not
   registered).
4. **Serial and cross-station dependence untreated** (inherited); the
   independent-samples CIs are approximate under weather persistence.
5. **B is not a mechanism finding**: it names uncertainty, deliberately.
