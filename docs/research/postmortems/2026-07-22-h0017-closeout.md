## 2. The controls tell one coherent story

- **Locked-NO control:** decided contracts priced at a mean **0.5¢**
  post-event (n=76) — consistent with H0007's earlier null on the
  locked side.
- **Placebo vs event reaction:** mean |Δprice| around the placebo time
  (12:35 ET, 9.3¢) *exceeds* the event reaction (16:35 ET, 6.0¢). The
  preliminary is not news: by 16:35 the day's maximum is largely
  realized and continuously observable, and prices have already
  adjusted. The event-study path (fig1) shows the locked control
  collapsing to ~0.5¢ *before* the official publication.
- **Pre-window calibration:** D = +4.4pp [−0.2, +9.1] — prices were
  roughly aligned even before publication (descriptive only; note the
  selection subtlety: at-risk identity uses the preliminary value, so
  pre-window rows are conditioned on information contemporaneous with,
  not prior to, the classification).
- **Per-variable:** tmax D = +0.5pp [−4.3, +5.4]; tmin D = +4.1pp
  [−2.7, +10.9] — tmin (the higher-revision-risk variable) trades
  slightly richer, directionally consistent with an uncertainty premium,
  not with neglect.
- **Sensitivities:** trade-price +2.8pp; window shifts ±15m +2.4/+2.8pp
  — stable. The extended-to-12h variant (+42.6pp) is a **measurement
  artifact**: near expiry, stale one-sided quotes make midpoints
  meaningless (bid 0 / ask high); the frozen +120min window and the
  trade-price sensitivity are the valid instruments, and this artifact
  is a caution for any future end-of-life price measurement.
- **Liquidity context:** mean post-window spread 2.8¢; 10,840 contracts
  of in-window candle volume across the cohort — real but modest depth.

## 3. Interpretation

Combined with H0007 (no locked-side mispricing), the near-settlement
picture is now consistent and closed: **the one information advantage
the label-formation research program proved (known, predictable
revision risk) is already in NYC market prices.** The mildly positive
residual (+2.3pp, n.s.) and the tmin premium suggest, if anything,
slight richness — the opposite of the exploitable-anchoring
hypothesis. There is no Phase-6 case from this channel on this
evidence.

Scientifically this is the program working as intended: a founded,
pre-registered, adequately-gated efficiency test returning a clean
equivalence result. Per RESEARCH.md, this well-powered null redirects
effort rather than disappointing it: the remaining edge candidates are
the *forecast-side* questions (H0003 → M-01 → H0006 in the roadmap),
which concern horizons hours-to-days before settlement where
information is genuinely probabilistic — not the near-settlement zone,
which traders demonstrably handle well.

## 4. Limitations

1. **Window and cohort**: one station (coverage-forced), 69 dates, one
   summer window; conclusions dated (re-run window per RESEARCH.md #9:
   ≥ +2 accumulation months) and NYC-scoped.
2. **Nine events**: the realized-rate arm is thin; the CI accounts for
   it, but a 3× cohort (available ~Oct 2026) would shrink the margin
   question further.
3. **Quote-midpoint proxy**: 2.8¢ mean spreads bound how precisely
   "price" measures belief; the trade-price sensitivity agrees.
4. **Same-date dependence** between tmax and tmin units: disclosed,
   untreated; physically near-independent.
5. **Equivalence margin is economic, not statistical dogma**: ±7.5pp
   was frozen as materiality vs fee/spread scale; a finer margin needs
   the larger cohort.
