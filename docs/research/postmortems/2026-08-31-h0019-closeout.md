# H0019 closeout — weather vs market held-out replication: NON-EVALUABLE

Date: 2026-08-31. Pre-registered outcome: **none**. **H0019 was never
executed.** No model was fitted, no test-set prediction was produced, no Brier
score or confidence interval was computed, and **no hypothesis verdict exists**
— not CONFIRMED, not SUPPORTED BUT INCONCLUSIVE, not NOT SUPPORTED, not INVALID.

Terminal classification: **EVIDENCE_ACQUISITION_FAILURE / NON_EVALUABLE.** This
is not a statistical result and must never be cited as one. It is an exogenous
failure: an upstream settlement-source regime change made the registered test
evidence requirement unattainable during the frozen test window.

> **H0019 terminated without evaluation because an upstream settlement-source
> regime change reduced the maximum attainable test evidence under the frozen
> protocol below the registered minimum; no hypothesis verdict was produced.**

## 2. What did NOT cause this

**Not a parser or implementation failure.** Completion coverage is **1.0 —
900 of 900** eligible settled markets produced a complete decision-grain row.
Of the resolved specs, 100% carry `rules_text` provenance under parser v2, and
**zero** Weather Company markets were admitted as resolved. The pipeline is
working exactly as designed.

**Not a data-collection failure.** 2,952 candidate markets exist in the test
window; they were collected. Collection gaps on **2026-08-14** and
**2026-08-23** are real but **non-binding** — see §4.

**Not a hypothesis result.** Nothing about weather-versus-market predictive
value was measured, because no model was ever fitted.

## 3. What did cause it

Kalshi migrated daily-temperature settlement from the **NWS Climatological
Report (Daily)** to **The Weather Company** on approximately **2026-08-14** —
two days into H0019's frozen test window. A hard cutover, measured across the
preserved corpus:

| Test date | Candidates | NWS CLI | Weather Company | Resolved |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-08-12 | 240 | **240** | 0 | 84 |
| 2026-08-13 | 240 | **240** | 0 | 84 |
| 2026-08-14 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 2026-08-15 … 08-22 | 240–252/day | **0** | all | 0 |
| 2026-08-23 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 2026-08-24 … 08-25 | 252/day | **0** | all | 0 |

Only **2 of 14** registered test dates contain markets governed by H0019's
frozen NWS CLI settlement model.

Weather Company markets are **outside H0019's registered settlement model**.
They are a different settlement authority against a different observation
source; treating them as equivalent to CLI-settled markets would silently
change the data-generating process the experiment was registered against. They
were correctly excluded, and that exclusion is not a defect.

## 4. The collection gaps were not the binding constraint

2026-08-14 and 2026-08-23 recorded zero markets, from documented host-sleep
outages in the permanent gap ledger. It would be convenient to attribute the
failure to them. **That attribution would be wrong.**

The settlement cutover fell on 08-14. The test window opens 08-12. So the
maximum number of CLI-governed test dates that could **ever** have existed is
three (08-12, 08-13, 08-14). At 4 readiness stations × 2 variables:

| Clean NWS CLI test dates | Event-groups | vs required 25 |
|---|---|---|
| 2 (actual) | 16 | FAIL |
| 3 (perfect collection) | **24** | **still FAIL** |
| 4 | 32 | pass — but unattainable |

**Even under flawless collection, the maximum attainable evidence was 24
event-groups, below the registered minimum of 25.** H0019 was unrunnable on
settlement grounds alone, independently of the host-sleep problem.

## 5. What was NOT changed

The frozen registration, split windows, readiness thresholds, decision rule,
subgroup clarification, model spec, and experiment logic are **all unchanged**.
No test window was moved, no threshold relaxed, no Weather Company resolver
added, no partial-test-set run attempted.

The parser v2 repair (`7132e3f`, ADR 0026) restored the **intended** NWS CLI
eligible population. It was implemented and validated **outcome-blind**, before
any H0019 rerun, and it raised readiness from `NOT_READY` (zero event-groups in
every split, a broken pipeline) to `VALIDATION_READY`. It did not change
experiment membership in response to observed outcomes.

## 6. What remains valid

**Train and validation data are sound and retain their value**: 112 train
event-groups and 26 validation event-groups, all NWS CLI, all seven registry
stations, balanced tmax/tmin, completion coverage 1.0. If the settlement-model
question is ever resolved, this accumulated evidence is not lost.

**The hypothesis itself remains UNRESOLVED.** Whether point-in-time weather and
contract information improves out-of-sample Brier score relative to the Kalshi
market probability is exactly as open as it was on 2026-07-26.

## 7. Terminal status and execution lock

H0019 is **terminal**. It must not be executed under the frozen protocol, now
or later.

The project has no separate experiment-locking mechanism, and adding one would
require modifying frozen experiment code — so the existing lock is documented
rather than replaced. It is genuine: `run_h0019` refuses unless readiness
reports `READY_FOR_FINAL_TEST`, raising `H0019GateError` before any outcome is
scored. Readiness is `VALIDATION_READY` and, per §4, **cannot** reach
`READY_FOR_FINAL_TEST` — the test window has elapsed and its evidence cannot
grow. The gate that stopped the run is the same gate that keeps it stopped.

Historical readiness logs in `readiness_log.jsonl` are preserved unmodified.

## 8. Follow-up requires a NEW pre-registration

No successor experiment may reuse H0019's identifier, windows, or registration.
Any future test of this hypothesis needs a **new hypothesis ID with its own
prospective registration**.

That successor is **blocked on a prior decision** — whether this project
supports Weather Company settlement at all. That decision is scoped separately
in `docs/research/design/2026-08-31-post-cli-settlement-scope.md` (**DESIGN-0001**)
and must be resolved first. Registering a successor before it is settled would
be registering an experiment whose settlement model is undefined.

## 9. Limitations of this record

This closeout reports gate facts and settlement-eligibility counts only. It
computes no predictive metric, states no verdict, and takes no position on
whether the hypothesis is true. The counts in §3 use H0019's own `target_date`
split logic; the 08-14 cutover date is inferred from a hard 100%/0% transition
in rules text across the preserved corpus, with 08-14 itself uncollected, so
the precise changeover instant is bounded rather than observed.
