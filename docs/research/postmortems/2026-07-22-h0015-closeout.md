97.5–99.1%, AS-OF modal share 96–99.9%, value agreement 100.0% at both
evaluation stations). **G4 failed at LAX**:

LAX's daily maximum essentially never occurs at/after its own 17:00

decidability floors, so the separation criterion is unevaluable there
by design, and any-gate-failure blocks the whole run.

## 2. What the gate report itself shows (gate facts, not verdicts)

The published gate inputs — which are part of the frozen output schema,
not post-hoc analysis — carry striking structure:

- **DEN**: ~93% of days are late-max under its 06:00 cutoff, and ~91%
  of days revise — the pre-registration's boldest structural prediction
  ("nearly all daily maxima should be 'late' there and its revision
  rate should be high — far above CHI/NYC's published ~14–19%") is
  qualitatively what the gate counts show.
- **LAX**: near-zero late-max composition co-occurring with near-zero

**These are not confirmatory statistics.** No CI was computed, no
criterion evaluated, and under the frozen rules none may be. What can
be said is limited to: the gate counts published by the blocked run are
directionally consistent with the composition mechanism at both
evaluation stations, and decisively inconsistent with nothing. Anything
sharper requires a new pre-registration.

## 3. Why the design blocked instead of deciding

The frozen criteria (stratum separation, early floor, attributable
fraction) presuppose both strata are populated — a design inherited
from the CHI/NYC regime where compositions are 25–38%. The evaluation
stations turned out to occupy the *extremes* of the composition range
(≈1.7% and ≈93%), where stratum-separation criteria are the wrong
instrument: at extreme compositions the mechanism's testable content is
the *aggregate* prediction (R ≈ composition-implied rate), not the
within-station contrast. PREREG §8 anticipated exactly these two cells
as the G4 risks and chose blocking over deciding from vapor; §12 R2
named the heterogeneity as "itself the test." The design worked as
specified; its criteria were mis-matched to the regime it met.

## 4. Follow-up (requires a NEW pre-registration)

A successor (H0015b) should freeze criteria valid across the whole
composition range — e.g., per-station exact-binomial bounds on the
composition-implied prediction error |R_s − [L_s·p̂_late + (1−L_s)·p̂_early]|
with reference-transferred conditionals, or a monotonicity/ordering
criterion across all four stations with exact small-sample handling for
thin cells — and must disclose, as post-hoc knowledge, everything this
blocked run published (the G3/G4 gate values above). That disclosure
obligation is the cost of the peek the gate report constitutes; the
successor's evidential value is correspondingly reduced for DEN/LAX
aggregates and should lean on the full-2026 time-disjoint window
(available ≥ 2027-01-15) as its clean arm.

Deliverable notes forced by the blocked outcome: no effect plots,
decomposition tables, or reference-cohort tables exist — PREREG §9
descriptives are computed only after a non-blocked verdict, and
producing them anyway would be exactly the post-hoc analysis the
protocol exists to prevent.

## 5. Limitations of this record

- The blocked outcome carries no evidence for or against the mechanism
  beyond the gate facts in §2, and those are descriptive counts.
- The G4 floors (50/30) were set for mid-range compositions; with
  hindsight they encode an implicit assumption the evaluation stations
  violated. That is a design lesson, recorded here for the successor.
- LAX's ≈1.2% revision rate is itself a notable archive fact (the
  lowest of the four stations by an order of magnitude) surfaced by a
  gate input; characterizing it properly belongs to the successor
  study, not this record.
